# Mexico Hardware and Power Tool Listings

Use for hardware, workshop equipment, power tools, and their batteries or accessories sold to the Mexico market.

## Positioning and Spanish Terminology

Prefer the common Mexican term supported by the product:

- Cordless drill: `taladro inalámbrico` or `taladro atornillador inalámbrico`; use `rotomartillo` or `taladro percutor` only when the verified mechanism supports it.
- Cordless chainsaw: `motosierra inalámbrica` or `motosierra a batería`.
- Jack: `gato hidráulico`, `gato de patín`, `gato de botella`, or `gato de tijera`, according to its verified design.
- Construction or paint mixer: `mezcladora eléctrica`, `mezclador eléctrico para pintura/mortero`, or another accurate application. Do not call it a kitchen appliance.
- Sander: `lijadora`; specify `orbital`, `roto-orbital`, `de banda`, `de detalle`, or another verified type.

Avoid translating `heavy duty` literally as a performance guarantee. Use factual construction, capacity, motor, duty, or application details instead. Use `inalámbrico` rather than `sin cable` when natural for the product. Keep technical units compact and consistent.

## Shared Specification Check

Use this as an internal checklist, not an obligatory output schema. Collect relevant supplied fields; mark only requested/required or materially consequential unknowns pending. Omit irrelevant fields and optional unknowns from ordinary Listings:

- Brand, model, exact tool type, intended materials and applications.
- Rated voltage, rated power, frequency, plug type, no-load speed, and speed settings.
- Battery chemistry, nominal voltage, capacity in Ah, battery quantity, charger input/output, and whether battery and charger are included.
- Motor type only when verified: `con escobillas` or `sin escobillas`.
- Dimensions, net weight, materials, ingress protection, operating limits, and package contents.
- Warranty scope and whether Spanish instructions and safety warnings are included.

Do not combine battery values to create a larger voltage or capacity claim. Do not infer power from voltage, torque from wattage, runtime from battery capacity, or suitability from appearance. Distinguish tool-only packages from kits that include batteries or accessories.

## Product-Specific Fields

### Taladro inalámbrico

- Chuck/mandrel type and maximum size (`portabrocas` or `mandril`).
- Maximum verified torque in N·m, torque settings, speed ranges, reversible operation, and impact/percussion mode.
- Maximum drilling diameter by material only when supplied by the manufacturer.
- Included bits, sockets, auxiliary handle, case, battery, and charger.

Never use `rotomartillo`, `percutor`, `brushless`, or a maximum torque claim based only on product appearance.

### Motosierra

- Power source, guide-bar length, compatible chain specification, chain speed, oiling method, tensioning method, and intended cutting application.
- Verified safety equipment such as chain brake, hand guard, lockout, scabbard, or protective cover.
- Battery configuration, charger, spare chain, oil bottle, tools, and case included.

Do not promise a cutting diameter, runtime, kickback protection, professional forestry use, or one-handed safe operation without evidence. Avoid language that encourages unsafe use.

### Gato

- Exact type, rated load capacity, minimum height, maximum lifting height, lift range, saddle dimensions, construction, and net weight.
- Intended vehicle or workshop use and included handle, case, adapters, or accessories.

State rated capacity accurately; do not convert vehicle weight into lifting capacity or imply compatibility with every vehicle. Never describe a jack as safe for supporting a vehicle during underbody work. Where usage guidance is appropriate, state that the vehicle must be secured on a level surface with correctly rated jack stands according to the manufacturer instructions.

### Mezcladora eléctrica

- Verified application: paint, plaster, mortar, cement, adhesive, resin, or another material.
- Rated power, voltage/frequency, no-load speed or speed range, number of speeds, shaft/thread connection, paddle size, and supported mixture limits.
- Included paddle, wrenches, spare parts, and other accessories.

Do not claim a maximum batch volume, continuous-duty rating, food safety, or suitability for concrete/cement unless documented.

### Lijadora

- Exact sander type, rated power or battery specification, no-load speed in rpm or oscillations in opm, speed settings, pad/belt dimensions, orbit diameter when applicable, and attachment system.
- Dust collection or extraction interface, dust bag/container, compatible abrasive size, included grit assortment, and accessories.

Distinguish `orbital`, `roto-orbital` / `excéntrica`, and other mechanisms only when the motion is verified by the manual, label, manufacturer specification, or clear technical evidence. A source title or translated marketing image alone is insufficient when the sources conflict. Do not describe dust collection as dust-free. Do not interchange rpm and opm, and do not infer material compatibility or finish quality beyond verified use.

When an abrasive assortment is included, preserve the exact grit counts. It is safe to advise selecting grit according to the material and desired finish and testing first on an inconspicuous area; do not assign a grit to a specific material unless supported.

## Title and Content Priorities

Build titles in a readable order: product type, power source or core specification, strongest verified differentiator, important size/capacity, and kit contents when they materially affect the offer. Do not repeat `profesional`, `potente`, `industrial`, or near-synonymous tool keywords.

Selling points should prioritize measurable specifications, control and handling features, compatible applications, kit contents, and maintenance or storage details. Present safety features factually; never imply that protective equipment or safe operating practices are unnecessary.

## Mexico Safety and Compliance Review

Flag for confirmation when relevant:

- Electrical ratings, Mexican plug compatibility, charger labeling, and applicable NOM or other conformity requirements.
- Lithium battery transport, labeling, charger compatibility, replaceability, and included-battery declarations.
- Spanish operating instructions, warnings, guards, PPE guidance, and age restrictions.
- Load rating evidence for jacks and performance evidence for torque, speed, power, cutting capacity, or runtime.

Do not claim `cumple con NOM`, `certificado`, `uso industrial`, `grado profesional`, or a guaranteed service life without current supporting evidence.
