# Listing Compliance Check

Use this reference for compliance audits, regulated or higher-risk categories, and final publication checks.

## Data Provenance vs. Compliance Action

Do not use compliance labels as data-source labels. When provenance is relevant, use the canonical data statuses from the main skill: `CONFIRMED`, `IMAGE_READ`, `RECOMMENDED`, or `TO_CONFIRM`.

Classify the compliance action for material statements separately:

- **PASS / 可使用:** supported and suitable for the listing based on the reviewed information.
- **NEEDS_EVIDENCE / 需要证明:** plausible but requires a certificate, test, authorization, manual, label, or manufacturer documentation.
- **REWRITE / 需要改写:** the underlying fact may be usable, but the wording is misleading, absolute, ambiguous, or noncompliant.
- **REMOVE / 删除:** prohibited, materially unsupported, unrelated, or unsafe to retain.

Distinguish ordinary declared specifications from proof of certification, tested efficacy, or guaranteed protection. Follow SKILL.md's source-review rules: coherent supplied facts can support factual copy without an independent test for every attribute. A competitor listing, supplier marketing copy, or product image alone does not prove certification, measured efficacy, or safety guarantees. Rewrite overbroad promises at the supported scope rather than deleting every underlying feature.

## Review Areas

Check for:

- Medical, therapeutic, health, weight-loss, cosmetic, or safety claims.
- "Guaranteed," "100%," "best," "No. 1," instant-result, permanent-result, or risk-free claims.
- Unsupported environmental, organic, cruelty-free, material, origin, certification, or laboratory claims.
- Trademark misuse, counterfeit implications, unauthorized character/celebrity references, and misleading compatibility wording.
- Age restrictions, child safety, batteries, electrical specifications, chemicals, cosmetics, food, supplements, weapons, adult products, and other category-specific restrictions.
- Before/after implications, manipulated results, fake scarcity, false discounts, or promises not supported by the actual offer.
- Phone numbers, email addresses, external URLs, QR codes, payment instructions, or attempts to move transactions off-platform.
- Conflicts between title, images, description, attributes, variants, quantity, and package contents.

## Reporting

For an audit, use a compact table with: severity, compliance action, location, issue, reason, and safe revision. Distinguish severity from the compliance action:

- **Blocker:** likely prohibited, misleading, or missing a critical requirement.
- **Warning:** needs evidence or market/category confirmation.
- **Optimization:** clarity, search relevance, or conversion improvement.

State the target market and the date/source of any policy verification. If current official rules were not checked, say: `需在发布前按目标站点和类目核对最新 TikTok Shop 规则。`
