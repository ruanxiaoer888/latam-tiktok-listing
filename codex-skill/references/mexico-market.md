# TikTok Shop Mexico Listing Guide

Use this reference whenever Mexico is the target market or the user does not specify a market.

## Language and Tone

- Write consumer-facing content in natural Mexican Spanish (`es-MX`).
- Prefer clear, familiar ecommerce wording over literal translations, ornate language, or region-specific vocabulary from Spain or other Latin American markets.
- Keep common technical terms in English only when Mexican shoppers normally use them or when they are official model/feature names.
- Use `tú` consistently unless the user's brand voice requires a more formal register.
- Avoid accents or characters only when a platform field explicitly rejects them; otherwise use correct Spanish spelling and punctuation.
- Translate colors, sizes, materials, package contents, and use instructions consistently across title, description, attributes, and variants.

## Local Conventions

- Express prices in Mexican pesos as `MXN` or `$` only when the meaning is unambiguous. Never invent or convert a price without a supplied rate or instruction.
- Prefer the metric system: mm, cm, m, g, kg, ml, and L. Preserve original measurements and, when useful, add a verified conversion rather than replacing source data.
- For mains-powered products or included chargers, verify the relevant voltage, frequency, and plug before claiming Mexican mains compatibility. For battery-only products, use applicable battery specifications; do not demand an AC plug or frequency when none applies. Do not turn one unknown electrical detail into uncertainty about unrelated, clearly stated uses.
- Keep model numbers, SKU codes, trademarks, and registered brand names unchanged.
- Use Mexican shopper search intent naturally: product type first, then important verified feature, intended use or audience, size/capacity, and relevant compatibility.

## Localization Checks

Flag rather than guess:

- Mexican warranty coverage and responsible seller/importer information.
- Spanish-language instructions, safety warnings, labels, or manuals when relevant.
- Certifications, registrations, or standards for regulated products.
- Import, labeling, NOM, COFEPRIS, age, battery, electrical, cosmetic, food, supplement, medical, or chemical requirements.
- Claims such as `aprobado`, `certificado`, `clínicamente probado`, `antibacterial`, `hipoalergénico`, `orgánico`, or `protección UV` unless evidence is supplied.

Do not state that a product complies with Mexican law or TikTok Shop Mexico policy without current authoritative evidence. When exact requirements matter, verify the current official TikTok Shop Mexico rules and the relevant Mexican authority for the product category.
